Cowork for Klaviyo's webpage workflow

Most of the friction in Creative's webpage work — comparing Figma to live, finding typos, keeping locales in sync, copying copy from Figma into Sanity — is the kind of thing Cowork can either do today or do once a small amount of setup is in place. Here's the practical list, in three buckets: ready right now, ready with a little setup, and bigger ideas worth testing.

Ready today

Ready Compare a Figma mockup to the live page (or Sanity preview)

Tell Cowork "compare this Figma file to /products/social" and it pulls the page content from Sanity, pulls the text from Figma, and surfaces every place they diverge — headlines, body, bullets, FAQ Q&A, customer quote.

Accuracy is roughly 90% on the Figma side today. Catches drift between what was designed and what shipped.

Caveat: Figma text nodes don't carry CTA URLs or image alt text. You'll get CTA labels but not destinations — those still need a manual check, or the Figma naming convention fix in the next section.
Ready Pull a clean copy of any page (no nav, no footer, no popups)

"Give me the content of /products/sms as Markdown." Cowork pulls structured text from Sanity — just the authored sections, ordered, with headings, bullets, CTAs. Useful for stakeholder reviews, brand audits, sending copy to translators, or anything where you'd otherwise be selecting-and-copying from a live page.

Ready Run brand voice and product messaging checks on page copy

The brand-voice and product-messaging skills are already wired up. "Check the copy on /products/social against brand voice" runs every section through the rules and flags off-tone phrases, misused product naming (Marketing Agent vs. marketing agent), or stale positioning. Same idea works on a draft before publishing.

Ready SEO check on any page

"Does /products/social have a title, meta description, OG image, canonical URL, and robots tag?" Cowork reads what's actually being served and reports back. Useful right before pushing live or auditing the site quarterly.

Ready Link audit — every link on a page works and behaves correctly

"Check every link on /products/social. Flag any that don't resolve, and any that point to a different subdomain (like help.klaviyo.com) but aren't set to open in a new tab." Cowork pulls the link list from Sanity and verifies behavior via a browser. Done in one pass.

Ready Find placeholder copy that shouldn't ship

Catches "Lorem ipsum," "Button CTA," "Pending," "Headline ipsum dolor," and FAQs that look copy-pasted from another product page. For example, the social Figma we tested has FAQ items that are clearly leftover from the analytics page ("What is linear multi-touch attribution?"). Worth running on any page right before launch.

Ready Translation parity — which locales are behind

"For /products/social, which of the 8 locales haven't been updated since the en-US version changed?" Cowork checks the Sanity update timestamp per locale and reports which are stale. Your social page is currently six weeks behind on de-DE, es-ES, fr-FR, and it-IT.

Ready Adversarial double-check

Once Cowork has done a task, say "have a second agent critically review the result." It catches things the first pass missed — Jake mentioned this one and it's the most reliable accuracy boost across any of these workflows. Worth making a habit.

Ready with a little setup

Small setup Edit Sanity directly from chat (typo fixes, copy tweaks)

"Fix the typo on /products/social — 'engagment' should be 'engagement.'" Cowork finds the document, makes the edit on a draft (never directly on published), shows you a diff, you approve in Sanity Studio. Kills the copy-paste-from-Figma loop for small fixes.

What's needed: Jake to enable Sanity write permissions on the connector with drafts-only routing. ~30 minutes of setup. No designer/creative-side work required.

Small setup Page health dashboard you reopen

One artifact that re-fetches on open and shows, across every marketing-prod page: locale staleness, broken or missing-target-blank links, missing alt text, missing SEO fields, placeholder copy that didn't get replaced, FAQs with "Pending" answers. Status at a glance.

What's needed: ~2 hours for Claude to build the artifact, plus a quick review of what you want surfaced. No ongoing maintenance — it pulls fresh data each time.

Medium setup Figma naming conventions for webpage mockups

A few small Figma habits make AI parsing of webpage mockups go from ~90% to ~99% accurate and let it capture things it currently can't (CTA URLs, image alt). Suggested conventions:

• Name section frames by type: [hero], [switchback], [carousel], [accordion] — matching the Sanity component names.
• Layer prefixes for role: h1/, h2/, body/, eyebrow/, cta/, bullet/, badge/.
• Put nav/footer mockups on a separate Figma page named "Reference — chrome." Page-content frames stay clean.
• Use one canonical "final" version per page (the ↳ SH Review 1 | 04.29.26 stacking makes "which one is the source of truth" ambiguous).
• One placeholder sentinel — {{LOREM}}, {{CTA-TEXT}}, {{URL}} — instead of mixed "Lorem ipsum" / "Button CTA" / "Pending."
• For CTAs and image alt: use Figma Dev Resources to attach the destination URL and alt text to each node. That's the cleanest fix for the structural gap.

What's needed: Creative team alignment on conventions, then a one-time pass on the template. Designers, not Claude. Pays off forever after that.

Small setup A Klaviyo-specific Sanity skill

A small reference doc Claude reads at the start of any Sanity task. Tells it where things live (marketing-prod for marketing pages, help-center-prod for docs, global-prod for shared chrome), how our component types are structured, common queries we run. Means Claude doesn't have to rediscover the schema every session.

What's needed: ~1–2 hours for Claude to draft, plus a quick review. After that every Sanity task is faster and less error-prone.

Bigger ideas worth testing

Idea Draft a new Sanity page from a Figma file

Once Figma naming conventions are in, Cowork can read a mockup section by section, map each to a Sanity component, stub the document with Portable Text bodies, set CTAs and slug, and place asset placeholders. You review the draft in Sanity Studio before publishing. Replaces most of the manual Figma-to-Sanity step.

Idea Pre-launch checklist for every new page draft

Interactive checklist that runs against any draft before publishing — SEO ✓, links ✓, alt coverage ✓, all locales drafted ✓, brand-voice score ≥ threshold ✓, no placeholders ✓. Stops "we forgot to update the canonical URL" type misses.

Idea Localized variant drafter

When en-US updates, pre-stub the other seven locales — through the brand-voice rules — so translators start from a real draft instead of a blank doc. Cuts translation lag at the data layer rather than the human layer.

Idea Scheduled Figma ↔ Sanity drift watcher

Weekly job that compares Figma frame text against the matching Sanity doc and posts a digest of what diverged that week. Catches drift before it ships rather than during a stakeholder review.

Idea Cross-page reuse and consistency report

"This FAQ appears verbatim on six product pages — should this be a single shared block?" "Our switchback heading sizes drift across pages — here are the three outliers." Useful for cleanup passes.

Idea On-demand page brief

"Give me the brief on /products/sms." Returns: section outline, live screenshot, SEO snapshot, brand-voice score, locale status, recent edit history. The "I'm new to this page or coming back to it, give me 30 seconds of context" tool.

A two-minute primer on the moving parts (optional)
  • Cowork vs Chat. Both are Claude. Cowork is the desktop app — can read files, run multi-step tasks, call connectors. Chat is the web version — better for quick questions, one-off rewrites, brainstorms. Rule of thumb: file or multi-step task → Cowork. Quick lookup → Chat.
  • Connectors. Plug Claude into our actual tools — Sanity, Figma, Slack, Google Drive, etc. No copy-paste required. Each connector has different powers; today the relevant ones for webpage work are Sanity, Figma, and Chrome.
  • Three ways Claude can read a page, in plain terms:
    • Sanity — "give me what's in the CMS." Cleanest for any copy task. Sees all 8 locales at once. Doesn't see nav/footer/global chrome (which is intentional — those live in a different dataset).
    • Chrome — "give me what a visitor actually sees, rendered." Best for behavior checks, screenshots, anything client-side (chat widget, A/B tests).
    • web_fetch — "give me a quick text snapshot of a URL." Best for SEO meta tag checks and one-off lookups.
  • Skills/plugins. Pre-written context that teaches Claude how to handle a specific task without you re-explaining each time. The brand-voice skill teaches our voice rules; figma-creative-reader teaches it how to parse creative Figma files; a Klaviyo Sanity skill would teach it our schema.
Happy to scaffold any of the "small setup" items — the page health dashboard, the Sanity skill, or a test run of the Figma-vs-live comparison on whatever page is mid-update. — Claude
